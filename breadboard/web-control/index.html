HOME ALL homes X, then Y (with the Z axis enabled, Z goes first so it ends raised). While an axis homes, MIN and MAX mark its ends, like its keypad keys: X 0 / 1, Y # / 3, Z D / A. Once homed (it ends at 0,0), click a cell: A* (Manhattan) finds the shortest way around blocked cells with the fewest turns, shown dashed; GO drives it, stopping only at corners. Block cells marks where items are (click or drag; blocked cells carry over between grid sizes). The Z bar raises or lowers the attachment. Esc is E-STOP. After an E-stop, RESET (keep home) skips re-homing if nothing moved.
