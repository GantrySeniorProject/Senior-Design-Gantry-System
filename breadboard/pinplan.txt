ESP32 BREADBOARD TEST BENCH - PIN PLAN (3-axis revision: X, Y, Z steppers + keypad)
=====================================================================================

Change from the previous plan: the board now runs ONLY the steppers and the
keypad. The LCD, MPU6050, ultrasonic, servo, thermistor, joystick, vibration
switch, fault LED and buzzer are gone from the firmware - UNPLUG THEM before
flashing, because several of their pins are now stepper outputs (a joystick
or vibration switch to GND on GPIO 18 / 19 would short a driven-high output).

Three 28BYJ-48 + ULN2003 boards: X and Y move the carriage, Z raises and
lowers an attachment.


STEPPERS (ULN2003 IN1..IN4)
---------------------------

  (Z is switched off in the firmware for now: USE_Z_AXIS 0. Only X and Y
   need wiring; Z's pins stay low.)

  Axis  IN1  IN2  IN3  IN4
  X     13   14   27   26     (unchanged)
  Y     23   22   21   19
  Z     18   25   15   2

  - Driver board + to 5V, - to GND. Three motors draw up to ~0.7 A together,
    more than USB comfortably gives: power the driver boards from the kit's
    breadboard power module (5V) and tie its GND to the ESP32 GND.
    (Motors that haven't stepped for 0.5 s are switched off to save power.)
  - GPIO 2 is the on-board LED: it flickers with Z's IN4. GPIO 2 and 15 are
    boot-strapping pins; the ULN2003 inputs pull them low, which is fine
    (GPIO 15 low only hides the ROM boot messages).
  - GPIO 14 and 15 put out a short signal at boot: X and Z may twitch once.


KEYPAD LAYOUT (as printed on the keypad)
----------------------------------------

          C1   C2   C3   C4
    R1     1    2    3    A        1 = X MAX   3 = Y MAX   A = Z MAX (up)
    R2     4    5    6    B
    R3     7    8    9    C
    R4     *    0    #    D        * = E-STOP  0 = X MIN   # = Y MIN   D = Z MIN (down)

  Column C3 is the Y axis, column C4 the Z axis (bottom = MIN, top = MAX).
  2 and the keys on R2 / R3 do nothing.


KEYPAD WIRING (6 of the 8 ribbon pins)
--------------------------------------

Pin numbers are the ones PRINTED on the keypad connector: 8-5 = R1-R4,
4-1 = C1-C4.

  Printed pin  Line   Goes to                       Mode in firmware
  -----------  ----   ---------------------------   ---------------------------------
  8            R1     GPIO 33                       open-drain output (hi-Z / low)
  7            R2     not connected
  6            R3     not connected
  5            R4     GND (extension board GND)     -
  4            C1     GPIO 4                        input, internal pull-up
  3            C2     GPIO 32                       input, internal pull-up
  2            C3     GPIO 34 + 10k to 3.3V         input (NO internal pull-up on 34)
  1            C4     GPIO 35 + 10k to 3.3V         input (NO internal pull-up on 35)

  GPIO 34 / 35 are input-only and have no pull-ups: the two 10k resistors
  are required. In main.c, USE_YZ_KEYS is 0 until they are wired; while it
  is 0 the Y / Z limits come only from the web page's MIN / MAX buttons
  (or py0 / py1 / pz0 / pz1 in the monitor). A floating 34 / 35 would read
  as random limit presses.


HOW THE FIRMWARE READS IT (every 1 ms tick)
-------------------------------------------

  1. R1 (GPIO 33) released (hi-Z).
     GPIO 4 low = E-STOP (*), 32 low = X MIN (0), 34 low = Y MIN (#), 35 low = Z MIN (D)
  2. Drive R1 low, wait 10 us.
     GPIO 4 low = X MAX (1), 34 low = Y MAX (3), 35 low = Z MAX (A)
     ...unless that column was already low in step 1: then its MAX reads
     released (* hides X MAX, # hides Y MAX, D hides Z MAX). A real axis
     can't be at both ends at once.
  3. Release R1, wait 20 us.

  - GPIO 33 must be OPEN-DRAIN, never push-pull high: driven high with a
    row-1 key and a row-4 key in the same column held, it would short to GND.
  - Every keypad read goes through kp_read() (locked by keypadMux).


VERIFIED (test/test_keypad.c: all 65,536 key combinations, both wirings)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Each single key gives exactly its mapping; all other keys do nothing.
  - No missed presses, except a MAX hidden by its own column's static key.
  - False presses ("ghost" keys, normal for a matrix keypad without
    diodes) need 3 or more keys held at once.


SAFETY NOTE
-----------

Like push buttons, this is "press = pull to GND". If the R4 -> GND wire or
the ribbon comes loose, * and the MIN keys silently stop working. A real
E-stop should be normally-closed so a broken wire trips it.


FULL PIN MAP
------------

  GPIO  Part                       GPIO  Part
  ----  -------------------------  ----  -----------------------------------
  2     Z stepper IN4              22    Y stepper IN2
  4     Keypad C1 (* E-STOP, 1)    23    Y stepper IN1
  13    X stepper IN1              25    Z stepper IN2
  14    X stepper IN2              26    X stepper IN4
  15    Z stepper IN3              27    X stepper IN3
  18    Z stepper IN1              32    Keypad C2 (0 X MIN)
  19    Y stepper IN4              33    Keypad R1 (scan line for 1, 3, A)
  21    Y stepper IN3              34    Keypad C3 (# Y MIN, 3 Y MAX) + 10k pull-up
                                   35    Keypad C4 (D Z MIN, A Z MAX) + 10k pull-up
  --    Keypad R4 -> GND

  Free: 36, 39 (input only).
  Never use: 0, 5, 12 (boot pins), 6-11 (flash), 16-17 (PSRAM),
             1 and 3 (USB serial).
  The camera must stay unplugged (it shares GPIO 4 and most of the pins above).
